# Insights Summary
## Project 23: Music Streaming Trends Analysis

### 1. Top Artists & Streaming Concentration
Across **2,500 deduplicated tracks** totaling **75.893 billion streams** (average popularity **62.65 / 100**), streaming volume is heavily concentrated among Superstar artists:
- **Top 3 Artists:** **Taylor Swift** (`8,969.31M` streams), **Bad Bunny** (`8,318.66M`), and **Drake** (`7,925.43M`).
- **Top 10 Artists** (Taylor Swift, Bad Bunny, Drake, Ed Sheeran, Dua Lipa, Kendrick Lamar, BTS, The Weeknd, Ariana Grande, Coldplay) account for **56.39B streams (~74.3% of total platform streams)**.

### 2. Genre Dominance
- **Pop** leads all genres with **25,596.88M streams (33.7%)**, followed by **Hip-Hop** (`15,592.52M`, 20.5%) and **Latin** (`9,733.68M`, 12.8%).
- **K-Pop** (`7,292.08M`), **R&B** (`5,187.80M`), and **Rock** (`5,155.59M`) form the secondary tier, while **Electronic/Dance**, **Afrobeats**, **Indie/Alt**, and **Country** represent high-engagement niche segments.

### 3. Yearly Audio-Feature Trends (2015–2025)
- **Rising Danceability:** Average danceability steadily increased from 2015 to 2025, reflecting the growth of rhythm-driven Pop, Hip-Hop, Latin, and Afrobeats tracks.
- **Shorter Track Durations:** Average track length declined consistently over the decade, aligning with streaming-era listener retention and short-form social discovery.
- **Lower Acousticness & Higher Loudness:** Mainstream production has shifted toward brighter, louder, electronically produced mixes.

### 4. Drivers of Track Popularity (Correlation Analysis)
Pearson correlation against `popularity` reveals clear, actionable relationships:
- **Loudness (`r = +0.4001`)**: Strongest positive driver — louder, professionally mastered tracks achieve higher popularity.
- **Danceability (`r = +0.3261`)**: Rhythmically engaging tracks perform significantly better in playlists and charts.
- **Energy (`r = +0.2645`)**: High-energy tracks correlate positively with mainstream popularity.
- **Acousticness (`r = -0.3743`)**, **Duration (`r = -0.2127`)**, and **Instrumentalness (`r = -0.2088`)**: Negatively correlated with mass-market popularity, confirming that concise, vocal-forward, studio-produced tracks dominate streaming charts.
